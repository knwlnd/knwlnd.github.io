About

Throughout my career I've been drawn to operational problems that don't yet have a clear shape. The situations I enjoy most are rarely defined by a single technology or process. More often, they're characterised by ambiguity, competing priorities and systems that have evolved over time.

My approach is to make the work legible: understand how it operates today, clarify ownership, identify where complexity is helping or hindering, and design operational systems that remain valuable after the immediate problem has been solved. Whether the solution involves technology, process, documentation or organisational change depends entirely on the problem itself.

Experience across Apple, monday.com and modern technology organisations has reinforced a simple belief that now underpins everything I build: Technology is the medium. Operational systems are the work.

How I Work

Operational problems rarely arrive neatly defined. The first step is understanding the system itself before deciding whether the answer is technology, process, documentation or organisational change.

Understand

Build a clear picture of how the work operates today, where ownership sits and where unnecessary complexity has accumulated.

Design

Create operational systems that simplify decisions, clarify responsibilities and make successful outcomes easier to repeat.

Enable

Support lasting adoption through documentation, automation, coaching and practical operational guidance.

Operating Principle

Good operational systems reduce cognitive load. They help people understand what needs to happen, who owns it and how success is achieved without depending on tribal knowledge or individual heroics.
