# ADR-001: Position the Site Around Operational Systems

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

The previous website presented Kerry primarily as an experienced IT Operations professional.

While factually correct, it did not communicate the recurring pattern across Apple, monday.com, Vanta and independent work: understanding operational complexity and designing systems that make organisations work more effectively.

## Decision

Professional Home will position Kerry as an Operational Systems Engineer.

Technology is presented as the medium through which the work is delivered rather than the defining characteristic of the work itself.

## Consequences

- Experience pages lead with capabilities rather than employers.
- "Projects" becomes "Operational Systems".
- Case studies focus on reasoning, design and outcomes.
- Technology supports the narrative rather than defining it.
- Future content should reinforce operational thinking as the site's central theme.

## Rationale

Technology platforms will change throughout a career. The underlying approach to designing operational systems is substantially more durable and better represents the work documented throughout this site.
